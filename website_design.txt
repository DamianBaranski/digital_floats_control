### Website Design for Digital Floats Control System

#### Product Overview
- Detailed description of the system, its purpose, features, and benefits.

#### Technical Specifications
- Detailed technical specifications and configurations.

#### Installation and Operation
- Guidelines and procedures for installation and operation, with diagrams or videos.

#### Maintenance and Troubleshooting
- Maintenance schedules, troubleshooting steps, and diagnostic tools.

#### Certification and Compliance
- Compliance with industry standards and certifications.

#### Spare Parts and Logistics
- Catalog of spare parts, ordering information, and logistics support.

#### Modifications and Updates
- Information on system updates and modifications.

#### Training
- Resources for training operators and maintenance personnel.

#### Customer Support
- Contact information for support, including phone, email, and online portal.
- Warranty claim submission, including RMA number instructions.

#### Warranty Information
- Details of warranty coverage, terms, conditions, and exclusions.
- Sample warranty certificate.

#### Documentation
- Access to downloadable manuals, datasheets, and other documents.

#### Bulletins
- **Technical Bulletins:** Updates on technical changes and improvements.
- **Safety Bulletins:** Safety information and alerts.
- **Service Bulletins:** Notifications about maintenance, service updates, or recalls.
- **Regulatory Bulletins:** Updates on compliance with new regulations.
- **Product Bulletins:** Announcements about new features or releases.

### Functional Requirements
- **Responsive Design:** Accessible on various devices.
- **Search Functionality:** Quick access to specific information.
- **Secure Access:** Secure login for authorized users.
- **Interactive Elements:** Forms, chat support, and feedback options.
- **Regular Updates:** Up-to-date content with the latest information.
- **Archive Access:** Access to past bulletins.
- **Subscription Service:** Subscribe to bulletins via email or SMS.
- **Search and Filter:** Options for finding specific bulletins.
- **Downloadable Content:** Bulletins in downloadable formats.
- **Feedback Mechanism:** User feedback or questions about bulletins.
