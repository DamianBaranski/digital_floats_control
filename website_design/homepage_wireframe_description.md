# Skymatik Aero Homepage Wireframe Description

## Header Section
- **Top Bar:** Contact information, language selector, and login/register buttons
- **Main Navigation:** Logo (left), navigation menu (center), search icon (right)
- **Navigation Items:** Home, Products, Support, Documentation, Bulletins, About Us, Contact

## Hero Section
- Full-width banner with high-quality aircraft image (preferably seaplane/floatplane)
- Blue gradient overlay with subtle grid pattern (matching manual cover aesthetic)
- Bold, white heading: "PRECISION AVIATION SOLUTIONS"
- Subheading: Brief tagline about innovation and reliability
- Call-to-action button: "Explore Our Products"

## Featured Products Carousel
- Horizontally scrolling carousel showcasing 3-4 products at a time
- Each product card includes:
  - Product image
  - Product name in bold typography
  - Brief one-line description
  - "Learn More" button
- "Digital Floats Controller" should be prominently featured
- Left/right navigation arrows
- Small indicator dots showing carousel position

## Value Proposition Section
- Three-column layout with icons
- Each column highlighting a key company value:
  - **Quality:** Emphasis on precision engineering
  - **Support:** Commitment to customer service
  - **Innovation:** Focus on cutting-edge technology
- Short descriptive paragraph under each icon
- Consistent with blue color scheme

## Latest Bulletins & News
- Two-column section
- Left column: "Latest Bulletins" with 3-4 recent bulletin titles and dates
- Right column: "Company News" with 2-3 news items with thumbnails
- "View All" buttons for both columns
- Critical safety bulletins highlighted with a special indicator

## Quick Support Access
- Background with slightly darker blue
- Heading: "Need Assistance?"
- Three or four support options with icons:
  - Technical Support
  - Documentation
  - FAQ
  - Warranty Claims
- Each with a brief description and direct link

## Testimonials/Case Studies
- Carousel or static display of customer testimonials
- Focus on professional aviation companies using Skymatik products
- Brief quotes with customer name and company
- Small aircraft icons or relevant imagery

## Footer
- **Top Footer:** 
  - Newsletter subscription form
  - Social media icons
  - Quick links to main sections
- **Main Footer:** 
  - Company logo
  - Contact information
  - Navigation links organized in columns
  - Copyright information
  - "PRECISION AVIATION SOLUTIONS" tagline
  - Privacy policy and terms links

## Responsive Considerations
- Hero image scales appropriately on mobile
- Navigation collapses to hamburger menu
- Carousel becomes single-item view on small screens
- Columns stack vertically on mobile devices

## Color & Typography Notes
- Predominant colors: Deep blues (matching manual cover gradient)
- Accent colors: White for text, light blue for highlights
- Typography: Bold sans-serif for headings, clean readable font for body text
- Consistent use of the grid pattern overlay on section backgrounds
